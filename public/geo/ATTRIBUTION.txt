Data sources for the smart log reader
=====================================

cty.dat  - CTY country file by Jim Reisert AD1C, https://www.country-files.com/
           Freely distributable; update by replacing public/cty.dat with the latest release.

geo/zones.json, geo/states.json - simplified from Wavelog GeoJSON boundaries
           (assets/json/geojson, https://github.com/wavelog/wavelog), rebuilt with tools/build-geo.mjs.
           Used under the MIT licence reproduced below.

geo/countries.json - simplified from Natural Earth 1:50m admin-0 map units (https://www.naturalearthdata.com),
           public domain. Rebuilt with tools/build-geo.mjs.
